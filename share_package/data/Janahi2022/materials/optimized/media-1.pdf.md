Supplementary Figure S6: Expanded GPR Nomogram. a GPR model trained with mean bilateral HV of male subjects in the age range 45-82 (grey circles) and then generated nomograms for the age range 30-100. Within training data range nomogram follows data reasonably well. Outside data rage, nomogram flairs out from expected range after 2-6 years. Fairing is faster in the lower ages because, outside the data range, the GPR model reverts to a normal distribution with zero mean.
