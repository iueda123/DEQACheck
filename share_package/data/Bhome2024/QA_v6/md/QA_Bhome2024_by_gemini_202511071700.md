## Additional Comments

**Additional Comments**: A major strength of this study is its use of a large, pre-existing normative model and its application to clinically well-defined patient groups, including a novel stratification of PD patients by dementia risk. The direct comparison with conventional GLM analysis, which fails to find a difference that the normative modeling approach successfully identifies, provides a compelling demonstration of the method's value in heterogeneous diseases. The handling of potential outliers in the primary outcome measure through sensitivity analysis is also a mark of high-quality research.
