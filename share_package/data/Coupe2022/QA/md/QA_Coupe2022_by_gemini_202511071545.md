## Additional Comments

**Additional Comments**: The paper provides a comprehensive description of the proposed HAVAs method, including detailed preprocessing steps, model selection criteria, and extensive external validation. While specific diagnostic criteria for patient groups are referenced externally, and detailed imaging parameters are not provided, the overall clarity and reproducibility aspects are strong. The paper also clearly highlights the advantages of their multi-model normative approach over single-model normative modeling, classical ML, and deep learning methods.
