## Additional Comments

**Additional Comments**: This study builds hybrid CN/AD lifespan models for selected structures and combines them into HAVAs. It emphasizes external validation and compares with normative, ML, and DL baselines. Acquisition parameters and clinical covariates are not detailed; harmonization across multi-site data is not addressed beyond ICV and age modeling.
