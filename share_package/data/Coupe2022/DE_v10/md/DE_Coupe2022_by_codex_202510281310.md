- **Association Analysis**: Diagnostic and prognostic classification evaluated via BACC, SPE, SEN, and AUC; HAVAs compared against single-structure normative models, deep learning baselines (ClinicaDL), and classical ML (SVM RBF, LASSO logistic regression).
- **Key Findings Brief**: HAVAs outperforms single-structure normative models and achieves strong external diagnostic performance (AUC 94–96%) and reasonable prognostic performance for pMCI vs sMCI (AUC 78%).
- **Key Findings Detailed**: External diagnosis: AIBL (CN/AD) HAVAs BACC 88%, SPE 93%, SEN 83%, AUC 94%; MIRIAD (CN/AD) HAVAs BACC 89%, SPE 87%, SEN 91%, AUC 96%. Prognosis on ADNI (sMCI vs pMCI): HAVAs BACC 73%, SPE 72%, SEN 74%, AUC 78%.
- **Key Limitations**: No explicit modeling of site/scanner effects; training structure screening includes performance on non-independent ADNI training data (risk of optimistic bias). Overall CN age mean/SD not reported.
- **Application Notes**: HAVAs is simple and interpretable (sum of hippocampus and amygdala volumes minus inferior lateral ventricle volume, all z-scored by %ICV).

## General Notes

**General Notes**: Training datasets (CN/AD): C-MIND; NDAR; ABIDE; ICBM; IXI; OASIS; ADNI. External validation: AIBL; MIRIAD; ADNI (sMCI/pMCI). CN age range ~0.74–94 years. QC pipeline combines automatic RegQCNet with expert visual review. Normative baseline flagged abnormality at z>|2|; main method uses HAVAs score based on CN/AD lifespan trajectory distances.
