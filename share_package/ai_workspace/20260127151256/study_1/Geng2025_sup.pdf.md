Figure S3 . The correlations between original GMV-lateralization deviations and  5-fold  cross  validation  GMV-lateralization  deviations  in  each  brain region. Normative models were retrained based-on each 5-fold TD children. The cross validation GMV-lateralization deviations in each fold were obtained from the re-trained  normative  model  and  were  used  to  calculate  the  correlations  with original GMV lateralization deviations. All correlations were higher than 0.948 and statistically significant after Bonferroni correction (corrected p &lt; 0.05).
