Additional  analysis  1. We  included  medication  in  the  linear  mixed  model  (shown  below)  to examine  whether  there  were  ACC  CT  differences  between  groups  on  and  off  medication (excluding  HC).  Medication  status  did  not  change  the  outcome  results.  Therefore,  we  cannot conclude that medication status affected our ACC CT results.

The following analyses were carried out.

ACC CT ~ 1 + Medication* Group*Sex +  Age + Edu+ (1 + Group | Site).

Where '*' is the Wilkinson-Rogers notation, Medication*Group*Sex indicates Medication+Group+Sex+Group:Medication+Sex:Medication+Group:Sex+Group:Medication:Se x

For the raw sample (381 first episode MDD, 118 recurrent MDD, and 524 healthy controls (HC)), no  significant  ACC  CT  differences  were  found  related  to  medication  intake  or  not (p =0.542, 𝜂𝜂 𝑝𝑝 2 =0.0009),  group:medication  interactions( p= 0.553, 𝜂𝜂 𝑝𝑝 2 =0.0008),  medication:sex  interactions ( p =0.841, 𝜂𝜂 𝑝𝑝 2 =0.00009), and group:medication:sex interactions ( p =0.602, 𝜂𝜂 𝑝𝑝 2 =0.0006). Crucially, the group:sex interaction was still significant  ( p =0.023, 𝜂𝜂 𝑝𝑝 2 =0.01).
