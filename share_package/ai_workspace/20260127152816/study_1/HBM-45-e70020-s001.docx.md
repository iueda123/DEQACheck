The inclusion criteria of T1-weighted images

1) Healthy or healthy controls in each dataset.

The Autism Brain Imaging Data Exchange (ABIDE)

The Autism Brain Imaging Data Exchange (ABIDE), backed by the National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH), aims to unravel the neurobiological mechanisms behind autism spectrum disorder (ASD). It includes two large-scale collections: ABIDE I1 and ABIDE II2.

The UNC/UMN Baby Connectome Project (BCP)

The UNC/UMN Baby Connectome Project (BCP) is a lifespan connectome initiative supported by the National Institutes of Health (NIH). Its primary objective is to document the neurodevelopmental and behavioral features of typically developing infants in the first five years after birth. The data are collected at two sites: UNC and UMN. 3

The Calgary Preschool MRI Dataset (CPMD)

The Calgary Preschool MRI Dataset (CPMD) aims to examine the structure and function of the early childhood brain, located within the Developmental Neuroimaging Lab at the University of Calgary.4

The Children School Functions and Brain Development Project (CBD)

This dataset was part of the Children School Functions and Brain Development Project (Beijing Cohort), which is a collaborative effort between the State Key Laboratory of Cognitive Neuroscience and Learning, Peking University, and Beijing Huirongguan Hospital. MRI data were acquired using a 3 T SIEMENS Prisma scanner in the Center for Magnetic Resonance Imaging Research at Peking University. 5

The Chinese Color Nest Project (CCNP)

The ongoing Chinese Color Nest Project (CCNP) aims to create normative charts for brain structure and function across the human lifespan. It consists of three phases: developing CCNP (devCCNP), maturing CCNP (matCCNP) and aging CCNP (ageCCNP). Only the devCCNP dataset from 2013 to 2017 was used, which was acquired at Southwest University in Chongqing, China. 6

The Healthy Brain Network (HBN)

The Healthy Brain Network (HBN) aims to develop a shared biobank by compiling data from 10,000 participants in the New York region, with the initiative being orchestrated by the Child Mind Institute.7

The Human Connectome Project Development (HCPD)

The Human Connectome Project Development (HCPD) studies aim to expand the coverage of HCP to include a more comprehensive range of ages across the lifespan.8

The Infant Brain Imaging Study (IBIS)

The Infant Brain Imaging Study (IBIS) aims to identify the early emergence of network functional impairments prior to symptom consolidation. This long-term, multisite study is funded by the National Institutes of Health Autism Centers of Excellence program. Scans were performed while infants were naturally sleeping. 9

The Multicenter Database on Perinatal Factors in Child Brain-Mind Development (PeriCBD)

The multicenter database on perinatal factors in child brain-mind development (PeriCBD) aims to construct a brain-mind model that describes children with adverse perinatal factors in the age group with a high incidence of neurodevelopmental disorders (NDDs) in the State Key Laboratory of Cognitive Neuroscience and Learning at Beijing Normal University. PeriCBD has conducted trial-data collection at two central district hospitals, the Anyang Maternal and Child Health Hospital of Henan Province and the People's Hospital of Liangping District, Chongqing.10

The Pediatric Imaging, Neurocognition, and Genetics (PING)

The PING data resource aims to create a comprehensive database for standardized measurement of behavior and image phenotypes, supported by whole-genome genotyping obtained from typically developing children spanning a wide age range.11

The Pixar Dataset (Pixar)

The OpenNeuro dataset titled Pixar was acquired using a 3 Tesla Siemens Tim Trio scanner located at the Athinoula A. Martinos Imaging Center at Massachusetts Institute of Technology. Participants were instructed to stay quiet and watch Disney Pixar’s “Partly Cloudy” during the scan. 12
