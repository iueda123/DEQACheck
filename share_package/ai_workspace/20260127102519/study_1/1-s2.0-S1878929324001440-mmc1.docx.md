Fig S1: Study flow chat

Study data is from the ABIDE I and ABIDE II collections of the ABIDE initiative (N = 2226). Only males and participants under age 14 years were included in the study, resulting in a study sample size of N = 816. The study dataset was then split into training and testing datasets. The training dataset was composed of 80% of healthy controls (N = 321), while the testing dataset was composed of the remaining 20% of healthy controls (N = 77) and ASD patients (N = 418). Further analysis with behavioral outcomes were performed only in ASD patients and analysis sample size was based on data availability.

ABIDE, Autism Brain Imaging Data Exchange; ASD, Autism Spectrum Disorder; BRIEF, Behavior Rating Inventory of Executive Function; CCA, Canonical Correlation Analysis; SRS, Social Responsiveness Scale
